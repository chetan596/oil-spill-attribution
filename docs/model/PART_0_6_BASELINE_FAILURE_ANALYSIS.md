## 6. Forensic Breakdown: Why V2 Produced Zero Detections

### Physical Preprocessing Root Cause
1. **Calibrated Sentinel-1 SAR Sigma0 Physics**:
   - Radar backscatter cross-sections ($\sigma^0$) over ocean water are measured on a logarithmic decibel scale ($10 \log_{10} \sigma^0$).
   - Typical open ocean backscatter values range between **$-35\text{ dB}$ and $-10\text{ dB}$**.
   - Oil slicks dampen capillary waves, lowering backscatter to **$-35\text{ dB}$ to $-25\text{ dB}$**.
   - Consequently, **100.0% of genuine radar backscatter values are negative numbers in dB**.
2. **The V2 Positive Mask Flaw**:
   - V2 executes historical preprocessing: `arr * (arr > 0)`.
   - Any value $\le 0.0$ is converted to $0.0$.
   - **Result**: Exactly **100.0% of valid sea pixels are set to zero**.
   - The neural network receives an input matrix of almost pure zeros.
   - At threshold $\ge 0.35$, V2 outputs zero positive pixels across all validation oil scenes ($\text{Recall} = 0.0\%$).

---

## 7. Forensic Breakdown: Why V4 Exhibits High Recall & High Look-Alike FPR

### The Dual-Edged Nature of Calibrated dB Clipping
1. **How V4 Fixes Input Dynamic Range**:
   - V4 maps $[-35.0, -5.0]\text{ dB} \to [0.0, 1.0]$ for VV, and $[-45.0, -15.0]\text{ dB} \to [0.0, 1.0]$ for VH.
   - This accurately preserves the contrast between oil slicks (dark, near $0.0$) and open ocean (rough, near $0.5 - 0.8$).
   - On Oil scenes alone, V4 achieves **$96.07\%$ Recall and $0.763$ IoU**.
2. **Why Look-Alikes Trigger 86.83% False Positive Rate**:
   - Marine look-alikes (low-wind zones, calm water, natural biogenic slicks, internal wave troughs) physically dampen capillary waves exactly like mineral oil.
   - In raw pixel-level backscatter, a look-alike has the same low dB value ($-28\text{ dB}$ to $-34\text{ dB}$) as petroleum oil.
   - Without wider spatial context (e.g., ship source proximity, slick morphology, texture gradients) or auxiliary oceanographic data (wind speed, SST), the single-scale $512 \times 512$ U-Net cannot differentiate low-wind calm water from an oil slick.

## 11. Evidence-Supported Findings vs Non-Established Causes

### Established Findings (Empirically Verified):
1. **V2 Inoperability**: Caused directly by negative decibel truncation ($arr > 0$). Validated by 100.0% data zeroing.
2. **V4 Oil Sensitivity**: Calibrated linear dB clipping successfully unblocks slick detection ($96.07\%$ recall on oil).
3. **V4 Look-Alike Vulnerability**: Look-alike formations exhibit low backscatter indistinguishable from oil at pixel level, causing massive contiguous false positives ($86.83\%$ look-alike FPR).
4. **Clean Ocean Distinction**: V4 successfully rejects pure clean ocean ($0.25\%$ clean-ocean FPR).

### Causes NOT Established:
1. Model architecture capacity (UNet-16 base channels vs UNet-32) is not established as the primary limitation (input signal representation and loss balancing dominate).
2. Tiling artifacts are not the cause of failures (Hann window blending produces continuous surfaces with zero edge discontinuities).

## 12. Pre-Training Action Items & Technical Guidance

Before any model retraining (e.g. in future development phases):
1. **Never use positive-mask truncation** ($arr > 0$) for SAR data.
2. **Preserve calibrated dB clipping** or standardize to zero-mean unit-variance per-scene normalization.
3. **Incorporate Look-Alike Balanced Sampling**: Loss functions and dataloaders must incorporate hard-negative look-alike penalty terms (e.g. Category-Weighted Focal-Dice Loss).
4. **Contextual / Multi-Scale Feature Aggregation**: Single $512 \times 512$ chips struggle on broad low-wind formations without multi-scale contextual pooling or auxiliary metadata (wind speed / SST).
