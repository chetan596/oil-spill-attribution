# Head-to-Head Model Comparison: `unet-dual-pol-sar-v2` vs `unet-dual-pol-sar-v3`

**Evaluation Date**: September 13, 2026  
**Evaluation Scope**: Dual-Polarization (VV+VH) Sentinel-1 SAR Marine Oil Slick Segmentation  
**Benchmark Corpus**: Zenodo Sentinel-1 SAR Oil Spill Dataset (40 verified scenes, 640 tiles)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This forensic evaluation compares the baseline operational model (**`unet-dual-pol-sar-v2`**) against the experimental retrained model (**`unet-dual-pol-sar-v3`**). 

* **Validation Threshold Selection**: For V3, an exhaustive threshold sweep was conducted across $\tau \in [0.10, 0.50]$ on the 7-scene validation split. The highest validation Dice score ($1.50\%$) occurred at **$\tau = 0.25$**.
* **Held-Out Test Set Performance**: Evaluated on 5 held-out test scenes (80 tiles of $512 \times 512$).
* **Scientific Verdict**: **V3 did not demonstrate sufficient improvement; model remains experimental.**
  * While V3 at $\tau=0.25$ detects significantly more true positive slick pixels ($\text{TP}=16,752$ vs $975$), it causes a massive influx of false positive sea-clutter pixels ($\text{FP}=2,184,434$ vs $12,786$), driving precision down to **$0.76\%$** (vs $7.09\%$ in V2).
  * At baseline threshold $\tau=0.35$, V3 severely under-predicts with zero positive detections across the test set.

## 3. Detailed Failure Mode & Performance Analysis

### A. Slick Recall vs Clutter Suppression Trade-off
* **V2 Characteristics**: Highly conservative. Maintains very low false positive rates across look-alike scenes ($0.0824\%$) and clean ocean ($0.0595\%$), but suffers from high false negative rates on dispersed slick edges ($\text{Recall} = 0.48\%$).
* **V3 Characteristics**: Greater activation on dark backscatter surfaces. While capturing more true slick signatures ($\text{Recall} = 8.16\%$), it fails to discriminate natural low-wind areas, biogenic look-alikes, and ocean boundary clutter from mineral oil slicks, resulting in an unacceptable $\text{FPR} > 10.4\%$ across unpolluted scenes.

### B. Look-Alike & Clean Ocean Specificity
* On the 10 dedicated look-alike benchmark scenes ($41,943,040$ pixels):
  * **V2 ($\tau=0.35$)**: Generates only 34,561 FP pixels ($0.0824\%$ FPR), preserving high discrimination specificity.
  * **V3 ($\tau=0.25$)**: Generates 4,384,682 FP pixels ($10.4539\%$ FPR), flagging extensive false alarms.

## 4. Operational & Deployment Recommendations

1. **Retain `unet-dual-pol-sar-v2` as Baseline Operational Model**:
   * Its low false alarm rate ($< 0.1\%$) prevents overwhelming maritime operators with spurious alerts.
2. **Preserve `unet-dual-pol-sar-v3` as Experimental Checkpoint**:
   * Stored at `ml/model_registry/versions/unet_dual_pol_sar_v3.pth` for ongoing research into boundary-aware loss functions, contextual feature conditioning, and multi-scale SAR transformers.
3. **Scientific Terminology Enforced**:
   * All detections continue to be classified as **"candidate oil-spill signature"** generated by an **"experimental model"**.
