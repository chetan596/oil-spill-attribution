# Section Prompt — Scientific Limitations & Investigation Follow-Up

Formulate the analytical limitations and actionable investigative recommendations based on the provided structured evidence package.

## Requirements:
1. Identify physical, observational, and computational constraints:
   - Sentinel-1 SAR spatial resolution (10m pixel spacing) and single-epoch temporal snapshot nature.
   - Demonstration MetOcean scenario assumptions (steady-state wind and surface current without sub-mesoscale eddy turbulence or coastal bathymetric interaction).
   - AIS transponder coverage, potential signal shadows, transponder deactivations, or synthetic demonstration catalogue constraints.
   - Diffusion parameterization uncertainty represented by the Modelled Origin Uncertainty Radius.
2. Outline recommended investigative follow-up measures:
   - Port state control inspection requests for top candidate vessels.
   - Oil sample chemical fingerprinting (GC-MS analysis) if physical slick samples are collected.
   - High-resolution optical satellite tasking (e.g. PlanetScope / Sentinel-2) over forward forecast drift sectors.
   - Cross-referencing coastal radar and maritime surveillance patrol records.
3. Attach the mandatory evidentiary disclaimer verbatim.
